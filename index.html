Stufa

A private, non-commercial app used only by its owner. This page brings the owner back to the app after signing in at a bank.

Privacy

Stufa has a single user: its owner. Through Enable Banking it reads the owner's own account balances and transactions.

This data is stored only on the owner's device. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising. There is no server and no analytics.

This page stores nothing. It only passes the one-time sign-in result to the app on the same device.

Terms
